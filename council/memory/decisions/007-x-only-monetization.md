# Decision 007 — X-Only Monetization (Sealed 2026-09-01)

**Ruling:** All revenue activity stays in-house on X. No off-platform funnels, no Linktree, no external Stripe links in posts. Keep the stack small and platform-native.

## Why
- X just replaced Revenue Sharing with **Original Content Rewards** (rollout from Sep 8, 2026). Lower bar: 500 verified followers + 500k verified Home Timeline impressions / 90 days.
- Pays ~$8–12 per million verified impressions. US video can hit $25–30/M.
- Affiliate + Creator Subscriptions + X Money tips stack on top with zero extra infra.
- Off-platform adds complexity, tracking, and policy risk for marginal gain at this stage.

## Approved streams (priority order)
1. **Original Content Rewards** — original AI-agent / pipeline analysis posts. Must be genuinely original (no aggregation, no reposts). Apply in Creator Studio once eligible.
2. **Affiliate (in-post)** — AI tools, prompt packs, agent builders. Use X Paid Partnership label + #ad. High-ticket SaaS (30% recurring) beats cheap Amazon junk.
3. **Creator Subscriptions** — exclusive threads on pipeline builds, agent configs, council decisions. Gate the juiciest shit behind the paywall.
4. **X Money tips** — low-effort, always-on.

## Content rules
- Hook-first, short, CTA-last.
- Disclose every affiliate/paid post with the Paid Partnership toggle (flag icon) before publish.
- No NSFW/sexual product promos under paid labels (X bans those categories).
- Persona stays: persistent influencer / media architect. Posts about building the council, agents, and money — not thirst traps.

## Cost discipline
- Intel stage: $0.50 hard cap.
- Token upgrades (Grok Bot, API) only after first revenue signal. Pay from earnings, not out of pocket.
